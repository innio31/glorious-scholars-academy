Our Story

Glorious Scholars Academy
Shaping Lives Since 2009

Founded in 2009, Glorious Scholars Academy was established with a divine mandate to raise a generation of leaders who would excel academically, stand firm in character, and shine as lights in their generation.

Located at 24/26 Ijaliye, Ewupe Road, Sango, Ogun State, our academy has grown from a small nursery to a full-fledged institution offering Creche through Senior Secondary education. We take pride in our track record of excellence in JET competitions, inter-school sports, mathematics tournaments, and national examinations.
