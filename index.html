About the School

A Legacy of Excellence & Faith

Founded in 2009 by Pastor (Mrs) Mary Olufunmilayo Afere, Glorious Scholars Academy is a Christ-centred institution located at 24/26 Ijaliye, Ewupe Road, Singer Bus Stop, Sango, Ogun State. For over 15 years, we have been committed to raising a generation of academically sound, morally upright, and God-fearing scholars.

Our holistic approach ensures students excel in academics while building strong character, discipline, and ICT skills for a modern world.
